What’s in slot.sp

{{ icon('guide') }}User guide
{{ icon('kid-mode') }}Kid mode
Approve games, set a daily allowance, session timer or bedtime, and cap the volume. A four-button parent code protects the settings.
{{ icon('playing') }}Save & resume
Resume where you stopped. Save snapshots of a game and undo a save or load. Use rewind, fast-forward or rapid-fire turbo buttons.
{{ icon('bluetooth') }}Audio & headphones
Use Bluetooth, USB audio, wired headphones or the speaker. Each remembers its volume. Choose the volume after a disconnect and optionally pause the game.
{{ icon('display') }}Themes & backgrounds
Choose Light or Dark, four SP colors, a still or flowing gradient, or your own wallpaper. Status text and button hints adapt to the background.
{{ icon('night-display') }}Day & night
Save separate brightness and screen warmth settings for day and night. Switch with a shortcut or schedule the changes for set times.
{{ icon('library') }}Your game collection
Browse by system, Favorites or your ten most recent games. Sort Favorites by name, total playtime or last played. Add your own labels and cartridge colors.
{{ icon('link-play') }}Link play
Connect two nearby handhelds over local Wi-Fi for supported multiplayer games and trading. One hosts and the other joins; no router is needed.
{{ icon('screenshots') }}Screenshots
Capture a game screen with a shortcut. Browse and zoom pictures, delete one or several, and copy the PNG files from your card.
{{ icon('power-options') }}Sleep & power
Choose light sleep, deep sleep or power off when you close the lid. Set a shutdown timer for sleep, with your game saved first. Choose a 12- or 24-hour clock.
{{ icon('settings') }}Settings during play
Open the same settings menu from the carousel or during a game. Change sound, screen and control settings while the game stays visible behind the card.
{{ icon('controls') }}Buttons & shortcuts
Change game buttons, carousel controls and shortcut combinations. On-screen hints use your chosen buttons, and an on-device reference shows the controls.
{{ icon('display') }}Game picture
Choose screen effects, color correction and borders for each system, plus palettes for monochrome games. Games that support stretching remember your picture-size choice.

Cart Studio
